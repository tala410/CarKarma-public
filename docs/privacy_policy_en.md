# CarKarma Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** December 7, 2024

## Introduction

CarKarma ("we", "our app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## What Data We Collect

### 1. Authentication Data
- **Email address** (when signing in with Google)
- **Apple ID** (when signing in with Sign in with Apple)
- **Unique user identifier** (Firebase UID)

### 2. Expense Data
- Vehicle information (make, model, year, mileage)
- Expense records (date, amount, category, description)
- Fuel records (volume, cost, mileage)
- Receipt photos (optional)
- Service reminders

### 3. Technical Data
- App version
- Operating system version
- Device model (for crash analytics)

## How We Use Your Data

### Primary Uses
- **Authentication:** To sign you into the app and protect your data
- **Data Storage:** All expense data is stored locally on your device
- **Synchronization:** When cloud sync is enabled, data is saved to Firebase (Google Cloud)
- **Notifications:** To send reminders about service and expenses

### We DO NOT Use Your Data For
- Advertising
- Selling to third parties
- Profiling
- Marketing

## Where Your Data is Stored

### Local Storage
- All expense data is stored locally on your device
- Uses iOS secure storage (Keychain for sensitive data)

### Cloud Storage (Optional)
- When sync is enabled, data is saved to **Firebase Firestore** (Google Cloud)
- Servers located in Google-selected regions
- Data transmitted over secure connection (HTTPS)

## Data Security

We implement the following security measures:
- **Encryption:** All data transmitted over HTTPS
- **Authentication:** Using Firebase Authentication (Google)
- **Local Storage:** Data protected by iOS built-in mechanisms
- **Data Minimization:** We only collect necessary data

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** View all your data in the app
- **Export:** Export data to PDF format
- **Delete:** Delete all data through app settings
- **Opt-out of sync:** Disable cloud synchronization at any time

## Data Deletion

### Delete Local Data
Settings → Delete All Data

### Delete Account
Settings → Sign Out → Delete Account

When deleting your account:
- All data from Firebase will be deleted
- Local data remains on device (can be deleted separately)

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

- **Purpose:** Authentication and cloud storage
- **Privacy Policy:** [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### Sign in with Apple
- **Purpose:** Authentication
- **Privacy Policy:** [https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Children

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
